# DuoLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

DuoLens is a camera, reaction-video and editing app. It does not collect, store or share your personal data.

**What the app uses**
- Camera and microphone: to record your videos. Recording happens on your phone.
- Videos and music you choose: to edit and combine them. They are read on your phone only.
- Face and background detection and speech-to-text for captions: these run on your phone, offline.

**What we do not do**
- No account, no sign-in.
- The app does not connect to the internet and does not send your videos, audio or any data to us or to anyone else.
- No ads and no tracking.

Your recordings are saved on your phone, in the Movies/DuoLens folder, and only you decide whether to share them.
